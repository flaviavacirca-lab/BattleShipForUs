How to Play

🎯
Take Turns

Tap a cell on your opponent's grid to fire. Hits show 💥, misses show •

🎲
Hit = Question

Every hit rolls a die. The roll determines the question depth: 1–2 Light, 3–4 Reflective, 5 Deep, 6 Vulnerable

💬
Answer or Skip

The defender answers the question. Mark it "Answered" to track progress, or "Not Answered" to see it again later. Skip swaps for another question at the same level.

💾
Progress Saves

Answered questions are remembered per player across games. Check the stats below to see your progress!
